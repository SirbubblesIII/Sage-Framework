## Appendix — The 28 CogFound Cognitive Elements

*From* Cognitive Foundations for Reasoning and Their Manifestation in LLMs *(Kargupta et al., arXiv:2511.16660). The "28 character traits" in your notes = these 28 reasoning elements, used as SAGE's external evaluation instrument.*

**A. Reasoning Invariants — "always-true" quality criteria held across steps**

1. **Logical coherence** — maintain consistency across reasoning steps and contexts
2. **Compositionality** — build complex ideas from simpler components
3. **Productivity** — generate indefinitely many thoughts/solutions from a finite element set
4. **Conceptual processing** — operate over abstract representations before linguistic expression

**B. Meta-Cognitive Controls — higher-order selection, monitoring, adaptation**

5. **Self-awareness** — assess own knowledge state, capabilities, task solvability
6. **Context awareness** — perceive and navigate one's circumstances, including other agents
7. **Strategy selection** — choose & explore approaches suited to task and domain
8. **Goal management** — establish, maintain, and adjust goals throughout
9. **Evaluation** — assess & adapt quality, efficiency, and progress of reasoning

**C. Reasoning Representations — formats for encoding and relating knowledge**

10. **Sequential organization** — order steps where sequence matters
11. **Hierarchical organization** — nest concepts in parent–child relationships
12. **Network organization** — link concepts through multiple relationship types
13. **Ordinal organization** — arrange elements by relative order or rank
14. **Causal organization** — connect elements through cause–effect relations
15. **Temporal organization** — order elements by before–after relations
16. **Spatial organization** — structure elements by spatial relationships

**D. Reasoning Operations — goal-directed actions on representations**

17. **Context alignment** — align to task and situational demands
18. **Knowledge alignment** — align to domain-specific structures & relations
19. **Verification** — check reasoning steps against pre-determined criteria
20. **Selective attention** — focus on relevant detail, filter noise
21. **Adaptive detail management** — adjust granularity to task demands
22. **Decomposition and integration** — divide problems, synthesize subsolutions
23. **Representational restructuring** — reformulate problems for new insight
24. **Pattern recognition** — detect recurring structures across contexts
25. **Abstraction** — generalize from specific cases
26. **Forward chaining** — reason from known facts toward goals
27. **Backward chaining** — work backward from goals to prerequisites
28. **Backtracking** — revisit and correct prior reasoning paths

*(4 + 5 + 7 + 12 = 28)*
